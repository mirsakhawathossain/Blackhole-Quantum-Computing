# Novelty: Diagnostic–dynamics hierarchy: chaos is easier than semiclassical bulk emulation

**Contents:**

  * Dimensions



Assessed theory: [theory-3](<../theories/scrambling-limited-simulatability-of-black-hole-information-tasks-revised.md>)

### Dimensions

### Phenomenon / Effect — Derivable Unstated

**What is Known:** Across the assessed literature, forward-only chaos/scrambling diagnostics and kinematic horizon analogues (OTOCs, level statistics, coarse spectra, analogue Hawking signals) are explicitly demonstrated at modest sizes and with relatively simple control in multiple platforms (SYK proposals/experiments; curved-spacetime lattice mappings; analogue horizons). The same corpus also establishes that higher-level “bulk-interpretable” tasks (robust teleportation-as-wormhole, high-fidelity TFD preparation, scalable decoding/interior reconstruction) either require substantially stronger control/time-reversal/entanglement resources or exhibit unfavorable resource scaling, and that some wormhole-like signals can arise in non-holographic/commuting models, weakening any direct inference from signal to semiclassical bulk fidelity.

**What is Introduced:** The statement elevates these scattered observations into an explicit, cross-approach empirical regularity: an operational hierarchy where diagnostics/kinematic analogues are feasible earlier (smaller size, looser control) than credible semiclassical holographic dynamics and especially scalable decoding/reconstruction. It further makes a near-term prediction about what quantum computers/analogue devices will preferentially realize first (diagnostic signatures before bulk-interpretable decoding).

**What is Novel:** The component facts (diagnostics are observable; clean Schwarzian/large-N holographic dynamics and decoding are hard; teleportation-like signatures can be non-holographic) are individually explicit and in places already effectively argued, but the cross-platform, law-like ordering is usually not stated as a single phenomenon-level claim in the papers and often must be inferred from resource estimates, finite-N limitations, and review-level comparisons. Overall novelty therefore lies primarily in the explicit synthesis/packaging of an operational hierarchy spanning multiple experimental/numerical approaches, rather than in discovering an entirely new diagnostic or failure mode.

### Explanatory / Mechanistic — Derivable Unstated

**What is Known:** Core mechanistic ingredients underlying the claimed difficulty gap are already explicit in multiple decoding/teleportation/scrambling sources: (i) scrambling diagnostics via OTOCs/operator growth and their experimental requirements (often including some form of backward/sign-reversed evolution), and (ii) Hayden–Preskill/Yoshida–Kitaev-style decoding and traversable-wormhole teleportation requiring two-copy entanglement resources plus conjugate dynamics (U*, U^T) and suffering from postselection-success vs depth tradeoffs and noise/depth sensitivity. Several works also separately emphasize that analogue Hawking/curved-spacetime simulators are primarily kinematic and do not implement information-recovery tasks.

**What is Introduced:** The statement elevates these ingredients into a cross-approach operational hierarchy: forward-only diagnostics and kinematic analogues are reachable at smaller scale/looser control than protocols claiming semiclassical holographic dynamics, and scalable bulk-interpretable decoding/reconstruction is generically bottlenecked by needing two-copy resources, calibrated subsystem partitioning/couplings, and (partial) inverse/conjugate dynamics, with severe postselection or circuit-depth growth and high noise sensitivity. It also folds into the same operational picture the caveat that teleportation-like signatures can be produced by non-holographic mechanisms in specially structured (e.g., commuting/integrable) models.

**What is Novel:** Most individual mechanistic prerequisites (two-copy + conjugate dynamics for decoding/teleportation; postselection amplification; OTOC/time-reversal control issues) are not novel relative to dedicated decoding/scrambling treatments, which already state them explicitly. The main novelty is the law-like, simulator-oriented packaging as a single organizing hierarchy spanning digital NISQ experiments, analogue engineered Hamiltonians, and kinematic horizon analogues; across the provided set this appears largely as a synthesis/unification of known mechanisms rather than a newly derived mechanism, though some of the statement’s stronger ‘generic’ claims about exponential scaling and broad noise sensitivity are not established in several of the cited experimental/proposal/overview papers and instead require cross-literature inference.

### Unification — Explicit Established

**What is Known:** A scrambling/quantum-simulator review already explicitly organizes the field across OTOC/operator-growth diagnostics, traversable-wormhole/teleportation demonstrations, and Hayden–Preskill/Yoshida–Kitaev decoding, emphasizing differing control/resources (including time reversal/two-copy resources) and contrasting kinematic analogue-gravity effects with holographic-information tasks. Many other papers and reviews individually unify subsets of these ideas (e.g., wormholes as quantum channels; decoding ↔ teleportation; analogue curved-spacetime simulation), but do not themselves present a single cross-platform feasibility taxonomy spanning diagnostics → demonstrations → scalable reconstruction.

**What is Introduced:** The statement packages these disparate threads into a single, operationally ordered 'diagnostic–dynamics hierarchy' claim: forward-only scrambling/chaos diagnostics and kinematic horizon analogues are systematically achievable at smaller size/looser control than credible semiclassical holographic dynamics and especially scalable decoding/interior reconstruction, implying near-term devices will validate diagnostics earlier than bulk-interpretable reconstruction. It also treats this as a cross-modality principle applying jointly to NISQ digital experiments, engineered analogue Hamiltonians, and classical numerics used to vet such Hamiltonians.

**What is Novel:** Relative to the existing scrambling-to-simulators review-level synthesis, the hierarchy is largely a relabeling/condensation of an already explicit organizing framework rather than a substantively new unification. Relative to most individual non-review papers (analogue Hawking experiments, SYK device proposals, wormhole/teleportation simulations, holography/QI theory), the explicit cross-platform, resource-graded hierarchy is typically not stated and would be at most derivable by synthesizing their constraints; however, the presence of an explicit established review that already performs much of this organization substantially reduces overall novelty of the unification claim.

### Generalization / Scope Expansion — Explicit Established

**What is Known:** Across the assessed literature, it is already common to distinguish relatively accessible scrambling/chaos diagnostics (e.g., OTOCs, level statistics) and kinematic analogue-horizon experiments from substantially harder holographic-information tasks (TFD-quality state preparation, traversable-wormhole/HP-style protocols, and especially decoding/interior reconstruction). At least one review explicitly covers multiple platforms (digital NISQ, engineered analog Hamiltonians, classical/numerical validation) and already emphasizes the kinematic-versus-holographic caveat in essentially the same cross-platform spirit as the statement.

**What is Introduced:** The statement elevates these distinctions into an explicit, modality-spanning ordering claim: forward-only diagnostics and kinematic analogues should be achievable at smaller size and looser control than credible semiclassical bulk emulation and scalable decoding/reconstruction, implying near-term platforms will validate diagnostics first. It also treats this as a single operational hierarchy intended to apply uniformly across digital experiments, analog implementations, and classical numerics used for validation.

**What is Novel:** Relative to many individual platform papers (single-modality analogue Hawking demonstrations, single-platform SYK/teleportation experiments, or idealized decoding theory), the explicit _cross-modal_ hierarchy is often not stated and would need synthesis/abstraction; in that sense the statement is a useful generalization beyond those works. However, the least-novelty evidence is strong: a dedicated scrambling-to-simulators review already explicitly articulates much of the same cross-platform landscape and the kinematic-vs-holographic separation, so the hierarchy (as a scope-expansion claim) is not genuinely new overall; its novelty is mainly in emphasis/packaging rather than new scope relative to that review.

### Constraint / Limitation — Derivable Unstated

**What is Known:** Across the assessed literature, it is explicitly established that (i) analogue-horizon/Hawking platforms largely probe kinematic QFT-in-curved-spacetime effects rather than holographic semiclassical bulk dynamics, and (ii) moving from scrambling diagnostics to information-theoretic tasks (TFD preparation, Hayden–Preskill-type decoding, interior reconstruction) faces steep resource requirements and strong noise sensitivity, often discussed in terms of very small postselection success probabilities and/or rapidly growing circuit/query complexity. Several works also already warn that wormhole/teleportation-like signals can have non-gravitational “false-positive” explanations in specially structured models (e.g., commuting SYK), so a signal alone is insufficient to certify semiclassical bulk fidelity.

**What is Introduced:** The statement elevates these scattered constraints into an explicit operational hierarchy claim about comparative feasibility: forward-only scrambling diagnostics and kinematic analogues are achievable at smaller system sizes and with looser control than protocols targeting credible semiclassical holographic dynamics and scalable decoding/reconstruction. It further asserts a near-term consequence for experimental prioritization: near-term devices will tend to validate diagnostic signatures well before they can realize bulk-interpretable decoding/reconstruction.

**What is Novel:** Most of the underlying limitation content is not novel relative to this corpus: decoding/postselection/depth constraints and noise fragility are already explicitly discussed in reviews and decoding papers, and the kinematic-versus-holographic caveat is explicit in multiple analogue-simulation works; the false-positive caution is explicit in at least one targeted analysis (commuting SYK). The main incremental novelty is therefore best characterized as a synthesized, statement-level “hierarchy”/feasibility ordering across platforms and task classes (diagnostics vs bulk-emulation/decoding) rather than new quantitative bounds or newly discovered failure regimes.

### Conceptual Reframing / Abstraction — Derivable Unstated

**What is Known:** Across the assessed literature, it is already common to distinguish (a) kinematic analogue-horizon/Hawking-like demonstrations from (b) dynamical/holographic or semiclassical-bulk-interpretable phenomena, and to caution that scrambling/chaos diagnostics (e.g., OTOCs, level statistics) are not sufficient evidence of a gravitational dual. Several papers also introduce strong but different organizing reframings (e.g., evaporation-as-qubit-operations, wormholes-as-quantum-channels, Hayden–Preskill decoding as teleportation/Grover), which supply many of the conceptual ingredients for the present statement’s lens.

**What is Introduced:** The statement’s added conceptual contribution is a single, explicitly operational ‘diagnostic–dynamics hierarchy’ that organizes black-hole-like simulation goals by resource/control requirements and predicts a near-term ordering: forward-only diagnostics and kinematic analogues become feasible at smaller scales and looser control than credible semiclassical bulk emulation (TFD-quality, large-N noncommuting regimes, decoding/interior reconstruction). It also folds an epistemic warning into the same organizing lens: some teleportation/size-winding-like signals can be generated by non-holographic mechanisms in specially structured/commuting models, so observing such a signal is not alone strong evidence of bulk fidelity.

**What is Novel:** Aggregated evidence suggests the hierarchy is largely a synthesis/packaging: many papers contain the ingredients (kinematic vs dynamical distinctions; ‘scrambling not sufficient’; separate discussions of teleportation/HP protocols and their resource demands), but do not elevate them into a single cross-platform, resource-graded taxonomy meant to guide experimental feasibility claims. However, parts of the cautionary content overlap with existing discussions that already emphasize diagnostic accessibility and insufficiency for holography, reducing novelty for the core ‘diagnostics easier than bulk’ message to “derivable”; and one recent contribution explicitly reframes teleportation-like diagnostics as multiply realizable (“pseudo-holographic”), which substantially anticipates the statement’s false-positive caveat.

### Empirical Synthesis / Meta-Regularity — Explicit Peripheral

**What is Known:** Across the literature set, many works provide individual “lower-tier” data points: kinematic horizon/Hawking-like signatures and forward-only chaos diagnostics are demonstrable in modest-size analogue and simulator settings, while high-fidelity TFD preparation, scalable decoding, and bulk-interpretable interior reconstruction are widely described as substantially harder and largely not yet achieved. At least one dedicated scrambling/quantum-simulator review explicitly assembles and emphasizes essentially the same “diagnostics first, decoding later” empirical takeaway (and related caveats), and a community white paper similarly notes the qualitative ordering (diagnostics accessible; faithful decoding/reconstruction hard).

**What is Introduced:** The statement elevates these scattered observations into an explicit, cross-platform meta-regularity/prediction: across digital NISQ experiments, analogue engineered Hamiltonians, and classical numerics, diagnostic signatures (OTOCs/level statistics/coarse thermal spectra and kinematic horizon analogues) will be validated at substantially smaller scale and looser control than credible semiclassical holographic dynamics or scalable decoding/reconstruction. It also packages this as an operational forecast about near-term devices preferentially realizing diagnostics before bulk-interpretable decoding/reconstruction.

**What is Novel:** The core ordering is not fully new in epistemic status because it is already explicitly articulated in at least one review (and qualitatively anticipated in a white-paper-style synthesis), so the statement is best viewed as consolidating and strengthening an existing community-level synthesis rather than discovering an entirely new meta-regularity. What remains more novel relative to many primary research and single-platform experimental papers is the explicit cross-modality elevation to a general predictive rule (those papers typically support it only as “derivable but unstated” from their narrow evidence), but the presence of an explicit review-level articulation is strong evidence against “genuinely new.”
