# Dual-Route Simulatability: Metric-Engineered QFT vs Holographic Scrambler Emulation

**Contents:**

  * 1 Theory Statements
  * Predictions
  * Conflicting & Unaccounted Evidence



### 1 Theory Statements

### Route-separation law (kinematic vs holographic information)

If a platform’s mapping encodes a curved background directly into local couplings of a low-dimensional field/particle model (metric-engineered QFT route), then the most direct validated outputs are kinematic horizon signatures (thermal-like spectra, redshift, horizon-crossing behavior, pair-creation/squeezing correlations) and entanglement across a fixed partition; but such a mapping does not, by itself, provide the resources required for black-hole information tasks (Page-curve unitarity, Hayden–Preskill decoding, interior reconstruction). Conversely, if a platform emulates a holographic scrambler (e.g., SYK-like) and targets OTOCs/teleportation/decoding, it can operationally validate black-hole-like scrambling and traversability without reproducing semiclassical Hawking emission spectra unless a separate metric/QFT mapping is supplied.

**Supporting evidence:**

  * Metric-engineered route: superconducting on-chip lattice black hole encodes metric via site-dependent hopping in an XY chain and measures thermal-like emission and entanglement dynamics. [Shi et al., 2021](<../extractions/extraction-quantum-simulation-of-hawking-radiation-and-curved-spacetime-with-a-s.md#c-001>)



  * Metric-engineered route: Dirac→XY mapping provides horizon/redshift/horizon-crossing phenomena depending on coordinate choice, with classical numerics and proposed hardware implementations. [Liu et al., 2024](<../extractions/extraction-simulation-of-the-massless-dirac-field-in-11d-curved-spacetime.md#c-001>)



  * Metric-engineered route: BEC Unruh simulator implements Rindler transformation via squeezing Hamiltonian and validates Unruh thermality and coherence/reversibility. [Hu et al., 2018](<../extractions/extraction-quantum-simulation-of-unruh-radiation.md#c-001>)



  * Holographic scrambler route: traversable-wormhole teleportation protocols in (commuting) SYK and SYK-inspired experiments target teleportation correlators, operator size/size-winding mechanisms, and TFD preparation challenges. [Gao, 2023](<../extractions/extraction-commuting-syk-a-pseudo-holographic-model.md#c-003>) [Byun et al., 2026](<../extractions/extraction-quantum-simulation-of-traversable-wormhole-inspired-quantum-teleporta.md#c-005>) [Bhattacharyya et al., 2021](<../extractions/extraction-quantum-information-scrambling-from-holography-to-quantum-simulators.md#c-011>)



  * Holographic scrambler route: digital SYK simulation proposals focus on OTOCs, time reversal, and steep resource scaling for Trotterized evolution. [García-Álvarez et al., 2016](<../extractions/extraction-digital-quantum-simulation-of-minimal-adscft-1.md#c-001>)



### Predictions

### 2 Likely outcomes

  * Platforms improving metric-engineered horizon fidelity (larger lattices, reduced reflections) will improve thermal-spectrum fits and cross-horizon correlation measurements but will not automatically improve decoding performance for Hayden–Preskill tasks unless two-copy/decoder resources are added.

  * Platforms improving SYK emulation (larger N, lower effective temperature TFD preparation, non-commuting Hamiltonians) will sharpen scrambling/teleportation diagnostics but will not automatically yield Hawking-like emission spectra without separate horizon mapping.




### 1 Unknown outcomes

  * A genuine hybrid simulator implementing both a horizon-mapped QFT sector and a coupled SYK-like scrambler sector could exhibit a controllable crossover between kinematic Hawking-like emission and information-theoretic decoding signatures, potentially enabling experimental probes of ‘greybody + scrambling’ interplay.



### Conflicting & Unaccounted Evidence

### 2 Negative experiments

  * Show a platform that claims to be metric-engineered (route 1) can perform scalable Hayden–Preskill decoding without adding two-copy/decoder capabilities; if so, the route-separation law fails.

  * Show a pure SYK-teleportation setup (route 2) reproduces a horizon Boltzmann emission spectrum without any horizon mapping; if so, the separation is too strong.
