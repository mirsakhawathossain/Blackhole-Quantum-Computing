# Dual-Track Black-Hole Simulation Principle (Kinematic Horizons vs Holographic Information)

**Contents:**

  * 2 Theory Statements
  * Predictions
  * Conflicting & Unaccounted Evidence



### 2 Theory Statements

### Kinematic-horizon simulations require metric engineering, not decoding primitives

In Track A simulations (effective-metric analogues), reproducing Hawking-like phenomena primarily requires engineering spatial/temporal profiles that realize an effective horizon (e.g., flow/sound speed or site-dependent hopping) and measuring local spectra/correlators; it does not require thermofield-double preparation, two-copy resources, or U* access. Therefore Track A feasibility is dominated by analog control over couplings and readout resolution rather than by decoding complexity.

**Supporting evidence:**

  * Superconducting on-chip lattice black hole implements site-dependent XY hopping mapped from a (1+1) Dirac field and measures an exponential Hawking-like spectrum and entanglement dynamics—without any decoding/TFD steps. [Shi et al., 2021](<../extractions/extraction-quantum-simulation-of-hawking-radiation-and-curved-spacetime-with-a-s.md#c-001>)



  * BEC Unruh/Hawking-Unruh simulator uses parametric pair creation to produce thermal mode statistics and coherence/reversibility signatures; no qubit-level decoding is involved. [Hu et al., 2018](<../extractions/extraction-quantum-simulation-of-unruh-radiation.md#c-001>)



  * Analogue Hawking radiation in BEC sonic horizons and related analogue-horizon experiments are discussed as feasible laboratory studies of Hawking-like emission/correlations. [Braunstein et al., 2023](<../extractions/extraction-analogue-simulations-of-quantum-gravity-with-fluids.md#c-001>) [Viermann et al., 2022](<../extractions/extraction-quantum-field-simulator-for-dynamics-in-curved-spacetime.md#c-003>) [Hu et al., 2018](<../extractions/extraction-quantum-simulation-of-unruh-radiation.md#c-005>)



  * Nation et al. proposal engineers horizons in SQUID arrays by modulating propagation speed; again an effective-metric approach rather than decoding. [Terrones et al., 2021](<../extractions/extraction-towards-quantum-simulation-of-black-holes-in-a-dc-squid-array.md#c-003>)



  * Dirac-in-curved-spacetime mappings to trapped-ion QRM and to site-dependent XY/Hubbard chains target single-particle curved-spacetime phenomena (redshift, horizon crossing/tunneling) without Hawking-information decoding. [Pedernales et al., 2017](<../extractions/extraction-dirac-equation-in-11-dimensional-curved-spacetime-and-the-multiphoton.md#c-001>) [Liu et al., 2024](<../extractions/extraction-simulation-of-the-massless-dirac-field-in-11d-curved-spacetime.md#c-001>)



### Holographic-information simulations require many-body entanglement and scrambling validation, not horizons

In Track B simulations (holographic/information-theoretic), success criteria are defined by many-body information observables (OTOCs, teleportation fidelity/mutual information, Page-like transitions, reconstruction maps) and require preparing special entangled states (often TFD/EPR resources), implementing two-sided couplings or decoding operations, and validating chaotic/scrambling structure. These tasks can be realized without any explicit horizon-like metric engineering.

**Supporting evidence:**

  * Traversable wormhole teleportation protocols rely on TFD and two-sided coupling; the white paper notes these ideas inspired experiments on noisy quantum simulators. [Bousso et al., 2022](<../extractions/extraction-snowmass-white-paper-quantum-aspects-of-black-holes-and-the-emergence.md#c-005>)



  * Wormhole teleportation protocol analyses in SYK and implementations (referenced) treat traversability as teleportation/decoding with two-sided interactions and two-copy structure. [Byun et al., 2026](<../extractions/extraction-quantum-simulation-of-traversable-wormhole-inspired-quantum-teleporta.md#c-005>) [Gao, 2023](<../extractions/extraction-commuting-syk-a-pseudo-holographic-model.md#c-003>) [Bao et al., 2018](<../extractions/extraction-traversable-wormholes-as-quantum-channels-exploring-cft-entanglement.md#c-001>)



  * Hayden–Preskill/Yoshida–Kitaev decoding protocols and non-isometric HP experiments are purely information-theoretic/circuit-level without requiring metric engineering. [Yoshida et al., 2017](<../extractions/extraction-efficient-decoding-for-the-hayden-preskill-protocol.md#c-001>) [Bhattacharyya et al., 2021](<../extractions/extraction-quantum-information-scrambling-from-holography-to-quantum-simulators.md#c-007>) [Li et al., 2023](<../extractions/extraction-information-retrieval-from-hawking-radiation-in-the-non-isometric-mod.md#c-001>)



  * Digital quantum simulation of SYK proposes qubit encodings, Trotterized dynamics, and OTOC measurement protocols as minimal AdS/CFT without any analogue metric/horizon construction. [García-Álvarez et al., 2016](<../extractions/extraction-digital-quantum-simulation-of-minimal-adscft-1.md#c-001>)



### Predictions

### 2 Likely outcomes

  * Experiments that improve spatial/temporal control of effective metrics (Track A) will enhance Hawking-like spectral/correlation fidelity but will not automatically improve any decoding-like capability unless additional Track B resources (two-copy entanglement and conjugate control) are added.

  * Platforms that can efficiently prepare TFD/EPR resources and implement two-sided couplings (Track B) will show improved teleportation/decoding metrics even if they have no horizon analogue at all.




### 1 Unknown outcomes

  * A hybrid platform could combine Track A (engineered horizon) with Track B (scrambling/TFD/decoding) to test whether horizon kinematics plus strong scrambling yields new cross-track observables (e.g., simultaneous Hawking-like emission and HP-style recoverability). It is unclear whether any such combination meaningfully approximates gravitational evaporation rather than a stitched toy model.



### Conflicting & Unaccounted Evidence

### 2 Negative experiments

  * Demonstrate reliable HP-style decoding or wormhole-teleportation signatures in a platform that lacks any two-copy entanglement resource and lacks any effective conjugate control; this would challenge the Track B requirements.

  * Demonstrate a Hawking-like thermal spectrum in a Track A device that provably lacks any horizon-like effective metric region; this would challenge the Track A metric-engineering premise.
